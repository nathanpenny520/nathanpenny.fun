Poem of html

Fun
Time stamp: 2026-06-30

从前有个人叫小高
他说写 HTML 感觉最好
但他写的代码结构语义一团糟
他写的标签谁也懂不了。

Once there was a man named Xiao Gao,
Who said writing HTML felt best of all,
But his code's structure and semantics were a mess,
And no one could ever understand his tags.
