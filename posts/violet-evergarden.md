<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Raised as a cold, emotionless <strong>"weapon"</strong> in a brutal war, a young girl named Violet loses both her arms and her beloved commander, Gilbert. In his final moments, Gilbert tells her, "I love you."</p>
<p>Unable to understand the meaning of these words, the war-torn Violet later becomes an <strong>"Auto Memory Doll"</strong>—a ghostwriter who pens heartfelt letters for people who cannot express their own feelings.</p>
