date: 2026-06-30
description: A visual poem about writing HTML.

<p>
从前有个人叫小高<br>
他说写 HTML 感觉最好<br>
但他写的代码结构语义一团糟<br>
他写的标签谁也懂不了。
</p>
<p><em>
Once there was a man named Xiao Gao,<br>
Who said writing HTML felt best of all,<br>
But his code's structure and semantics were a mess,<br>
And no one could ever understand his tags.
</em></p>
